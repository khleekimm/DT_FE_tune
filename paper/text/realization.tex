\section{Realization and queries}\label{sec:realization}
For the accepted full-column-rank map $T_\theta$, the FE-space receptance is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R_\theta(s)=T_\theta\Delta_\theta(s)^{-1}T_\theta^{\top}
 =\sum_{k=1}^{2r}\frac{\psi_k\psi_k^{\top}}{s-\lambda_k},\qquad
 \psi_k=T_\theta\phi_k,
 \label{eq:receptance}
\end{equation}
so that $u(s)=\mathcal R_\theta(s)Bf(s)$ and
$s^2S\mathcal R_\theta(s)B=H_\theta(s)$ for zero initial conditions. The
$\psi_k$ are the damped mode shapes on the mesh. Padding the modal matrix
with $n-r$ zero columns shows that this is a modal model on the whole mesh:
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R_\theta(s)=\begin{bmatrix}T_\theta&0\end{bmatrix}
 \begin{bmatrix}\Delta_\theta(s)^{-1}&0\\0&\Delta_{\rm c}(s)^{-1}\end{bmatrix}
 \begin{bmatrix}T_\theta&0\end{bmatrix}^{\top}
 \quad\text{for every invertible }\Delta_{\rm c}(s).
 \label{eq:trivial-modes}
\end{equation}
The $n-r$ unidentified directions are trivial modes of zero shape. They
carry no residue, and whatever dynamics $\Delta_{\rm c}$ is assigned to them
never enters a response. No nodes or coordinates have to be selected. Motion
outside $\range T_\theta$ is not represented.

The same model has mass, damping and stiffness operators on the FE space.
Choose any left inverse $V\in\R^{r\times n}$, $VT_\theta=I_r$, so that $q=Vu$
on $\range T_\theta$ and $P_V=T_\theta V$ projects onto that subspace.
\begin{theorem}[Realization]\label{thm:realization}
Let $T_\theta$ have full column rank, $VT_\theta=I_r$, and define
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal M=V^{\top}V,\qquad
 \mathcal C=V^{\top}\Gamma V,\qquad
 \mathcal K=V^{\top}\Omega^2V,\qquad
 \mathcal D(s)=s^2\mathcal M+s\mathcal C+\mathcal K.
 \label{eq:realization}
\end{equation}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] The three operators are symmetric positive semidefinite of rank
$r$, with common kernel $\ker V$. For $u\in\range T_\theta$,
\[
 \mathcal M\ddot u+\mathcal C\dot u+\mathcal Ku=P_V^{\top}Bf
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \ddot q+\Gamma\dot q+\Omega^2q=T_\theta^{\top}Bf,\qquad q=Vu.
\]
\item[(ii)] Wherever $\Delta_\theta(s)$ is invertible,
$\mathcal D\mathcal R_\theta=P_V^{\top}$ and
$\mathcal R_\theta\mathcal D=P_V$, so $\mathcal R_\theta$ is a reflexive
generalized inverse of $\mathcal D$ \citep{benisrael2003}, independent of
$V$. For any load matrix $B'$ and output matrix $S'$ the accelerance is
$s^2S'\mathcal R_\theta(s)B'$. At the registered ports it is $H_\theta(s)$.
\item[(iii)] If $\Pi$ selects $r$ displacement rows with $P=\Pi T_\theta$
invertible, then $V=P^{-1}\Pi$ gives $\mathcal M=\Pi^{\top}M_\theta\Pi$,
$\mathcal C=\Pi^{\top}C_\theta\Pi$, $\mathcal K=\Pi^{\top}K_\theta\Pi$ with the
positive definite
\[
 M_\theta=P^{-\top}P^{-1},\qquad
 C_\theta=P^{-\top}\Gamma P^{-1},\qquad
 K_\theta=P^{-\top}\Omega^2P^{-1},\qquad
 \kappa_2(M_\theta)=\kappa_2(P)^2.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since $VT_\theta=I_r$, $V$ has rank $r$ and $P_V^2=P_V$,
$P_VT_\theta=T_\theta$, $VP_V=V$.

(i) For $X\in\{I_r,\Gamma,\Omega^2\}$ the form
$u^{\top}V^{\top}XVu=(Vu)^{\top}X(Vu)$ vanishes only when $Vu=0$, because
$X\succ0$. So the three operators are positive semidefinite with kernel
$\ker V$ and rank $r$. For $u=T_\theta q$ the FE equation is exactly
\[
 V^{\top}\bigl(\ddot q+\Gamma\dot q+\Omega^2q-T_\theta^{\top}Bf\bigr)=0,
\]
and $q=Vu$. Injectivity of $V^{\top}$ proves the equivalence.

(ii) By Eq.~\eqref{eq:realization}, $\mathcal D=V^{\top}\Delta_\theta V$.
Where $\Delta_\theta$ is invertible, direct multiplication gives
\[
 \mathcal D\mathcal R_\theta
 =V^{\top}\Delta_\theta(VT_\theta)\Delta_\theta^{-1}T_\theta^{\top}
 =P_V^{\top},\qquad
 \mathcal R_\theta\mathcal D
 =T_\theta\Delta_\theta^{-1}(VT_\theta)^{\top}\Delta_\theta V=P_V.
\]
As $P_V^{\top}\mathcal D=\mathcal D$ and
$P_V\mathcal R_\theta=\mathcal R_\theta$, both generalized-inverse
identities follow. Under a load $B'f$ the modal response is
$q=\Delta_\theta^{-1}T_\theta^{\top}B'f$. So the output
$s^2S'T_\theta q$ equals $s^2S'\mathcal R_\theta B'f$. At the registered
ports $ST_\theta=R_{\rm o}W$ and $T_\theta^{\top}B=W^{\top}R_{\rm i}^{\top}$
give $H_\theta$. None of these responses contains $V$.

(iii) For $V=P^{-1}\Pi$, $VT_\theta=P^{-1}P=I_r$. Substitution gives
$\mathcal M=\Pi^{\top}P^{-\top}P^{-1}\Pi$ and the analogous damping and
stiffness identities. Congruence with $P^{-1}$ preserves positive
definiteness. Finally $M_\theta=(PP^{\top})^{-1}$ has eigenvalues
$\sigma_i(P)^{-2}$, so $\kappa_2(M_\theta)=\kappa_2(P)^2$.
\end{proof}

Since $VT_\theta=I_r$, the realized operators reproduce the fitted modal
model exactly:
\[
 T_\theta^{\top}\mathcal MT_\theta=I_r,\quad
 T_\theta^{\top}\mathcal CT_\theta=\Gamma,\quad
 T_\theta^{\top}\mathcal KT_\theta=\Omega^2,\quad
 \mathcal KT_\theta=\mathcal MT_\theta\Omega^2.
\]
The last identity is Eq.~\eqref{eq:intertwining} with $(\mathcal M,\mathcal K)$
in place of $(M_0,K_0)$. The realized operators satisfy it exactly. The
nominal ones satisfy it only as closely as $J_F$ allows along the measured
motion.

Part~(iii) is the square realization in selected coordinates $x=\Pi u=Pq$.
With $L=T_\theta P^{-1}$ it reads $u=Lx$,
$M_\theta\ddot x+C_\theta\dot x+K_\theta x=L^{\top}Bf$ and $a=SL\ddot x$.
Its conditioning is that of the row selection, not of the model.
Appendix~\ref{sec:state-details} gives the selection and the state-space form
with the acceleration feedthrough
$\lim_{|s|\to\infty}H_\theta(s)=R_{\rm o}WW^{\top}R_{\rm i}^{\top}$.
For the nominal-mass choice
$V=(T_\theta^{\top}M_0T_\theta)^{-1}T_\theta^{\top}M_0$, $P_V$ is the
$M_0$-orthogonal projector. For every left inverse
\[
 \mathcal M-P_V^{\top}M_0P_V=V^{\top}(I_r-T_\theta^{\top}M_0T_\theta)V,\qquad
 \mathcal K-P_V^{\top}K_0P_V=V^{\top}(\Omega^2-T_\theta^{\top}K_0T_\theta)V.
\]
So the realized and projected nominal operators differ by the Gram defects
of the completed map. These defects follow from the completion and are not
fitted.

A query names a frequency band $\mathcal B$, load directions $B'$ and response
directions $S'$. Its answer is the complex accelerance with phase
\begin{equation}
 H'(s)=s^2S'\mathcal R_\theta(s)B',\qquad s=\mathrm i2\pi\nu,\quad\nu\in\mathcal B.
 \label{eq:query}
\end{equation}
At registered ports it reproduces the fitted FRFs. Elsewhere it inherits
the accuracy of the completion. The inverse query takes known force locations
and measured accelerations,
\begin{equation}
 f_\theta(s)=\argmin_{f\in\C^p}
 \|D(s)^{-1}(a(s)-H_\theta(s)f)\|_2^2,\qquad
 u_\theta(s)=\mathcal R_\theta(s)Bf_\theta(s),
 \label{eq:force-query}
\end{equation}
where $D(s)$ is a positive diagonal matrix of channel-error scales. The
estimate is unique when $D^{-1}H_\theta$ has full column rank, and
$u_\theta$ is the displacement it implies on the whole mesh.
Appendix~\ref{sec:forces} builds $D$ from training residuals and evaluates
the estimate with two and with one active force.
