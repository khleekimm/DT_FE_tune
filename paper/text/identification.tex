\section{Step 1: fitting \texorpdfstring{$\theta$}{theta} at the ports}\label{sec:identification}
Step~(i) of Eq.~\eqref{eq:two-steps} is a regression of the model FRF on the
measured FRF with a regularization term:
\begin{equation}
 \min_{W,\,\omega>0,\,\Gamma\succ0}J(\theta),\qquad
 J(\theta)=\underbrace{\frac1{Nmp}\sum_{\ell,o,i}
 \left|\frac{[H_\theta(s_\ell)-\widehat H_\ell]_{oi}}{d_{oi}}\right|^2}_{\text{FRF misfit}}
 +\underbrace{\beta\tr(W^{\top}GW)}_{\text{regularization}}.
 \label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}
The misfit runs over all lines $\ell$, responses $o$ and forces $i$. Each
transfer is scaled by its RMS magnitude $d_{oi}$ on the training record.
Errors are reported as 100 times the square root of the misfit, in percent.
The regularization uses a roughness matrix $G\succeq0$ to penalize uneven
port rows $W$, with weight $\beta>0$. Appendix~\ref{sec:fit-details} gives
the scales, the penalty and the local solver.
